Packaging-environment limitation
--------------------------------
A full Qt/PJSIP GUI link was not performed here because the packaging container does not have the Qt and PJSIP development packages installed. The included all-platform builder installs/bootstrap those dependencies on supported targets. PJSIP 2.17 documents both multiple Account objects under one Endpoint and runtime STUN-server updates after libInit; r19 is structured around those APIs.
