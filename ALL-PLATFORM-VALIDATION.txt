Packaging-environment limitation
--------------------------------
A full Qt/PJSIP GUI link was not performed here because the packaging container does not have the Qt and PJSIP development packages installed. The builder is designed to install/bootstrap those dependencies on a supported target. The PJSIP next-hop fields used by r18 are part of the transport callback metadata; the source regression checks and non-Qt core tests pass.
