SIPHER 2.0 DID Intelligence rebuild — 2026-09-11

- SIPHER does not scrape or depend on SpamCalls.net HTML.
- Carrier output explicitly distinguishes NANPA registry assignment from live current-carrier/LRN data.
- r18 route/audit fixes, r19 multi-SIP/zero-account behavior, and SIPHER 2.0 unified `sipher` binary behavior retained.
